## 2. Platform App Settings for High-Fidelity Concert Video [22, 23]

TikTok defaults to heavy data compression, which ruins high-end festival footage and live bass transients [22]. Follow these settings to bypass aggressive compression:

### The "Allow High-Quality Uploads" Setting [22]
*   **Action:** You must manually enable high-quality uploads for *every single post* [22].
*   **Workflow:** On the final post screen before hitting "Post", scroll down to the **More options** menu and toggle the **Allow High-Quality Uploads** switch to **ON** [22].

### Raw Target Export Specifications [23]
*   **Resolution:** 1080 x 1920 (9:16 Aspect Ratio) [23]
*   **Frame Rate:** 30 or 60 fps (Constant Frame Rate - CFR) [23]
*   **Codec:** H.264 (MP4 container) [23]
*   **Bitrate:** 8–12 Mbps [23]

## 3. TikTok Copyright Mechanics & "Ghost-Linking" Protocol [23, 24, 25]

Managing copyrighted audio is essential to prevent content muting while maintaining high-fidelity, heavy-bass venue recordings [23].

### The 60-Second Audio Muting Rule [23]
*   Using copyrighted original sounds for clips longer than 60 seconds triggers automatic audio muting on the platform [23]. To avoid this, keep all clips between **15–45 seconds** [23].

### The Master Audio Sync ("Ghost-Linking") Method [24, 25]
This protocol allows you to index on official, trending music pages while keeping your high-fidelity remastered venue audio [24, 25].

1.  **Upload:** Select the video containing your crisp, remastered live venue audio [24].
2.  **Add Sound:** Tap **"Add sound"** at the top of the edit screen and search for the official studio track corresponding to the video [24].
3.  **Access Volume:** Tap the **"Volume"** button at the bottom right of the screen [24].
4.  **Adjust Added Sound:** Slide the **Added Sound Volume** down to **1% - 3%** (or 0% if the platform allows) [24].
5.  **Maintain Original:** Keep the **Original Sound Volume** at **100%** [24].

**The Benefit:** Your video indexes directly on the official track's TikTok sound discovery page (boosting algorithmic reach), while the audience hears your high-fidelity live bass, laser atmosphere, and crowd energy [25].
