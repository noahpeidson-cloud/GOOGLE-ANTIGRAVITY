## 4. Copyright Architecture & Content ID Safeguards [38, 46]

Managing copyright is crucial when publishing short-form clips containing commercial EDM tracks, bootlegs, and festival IDs [38].

### The 60-Second Threshold Rule [39]
Video duration dictates how YouTube processes copyright claims on vertical content [39]:
*   **Shorts Under 60 Seconds:** When raw festival audio under 60 seconds triggers a Content ID match, it generates a standard copyright claim [39]. This standard claim redirects ad monetization to the record label but allows the clip to remain visible globally without channel penalties or strikes [39].
*   **Shorts Between 61 and 180 Seconds (1 to 3 Minutes):** Under YouTube platform policy, vertical videos longer than 60 seconds that receive an active Content ID claim are **automatically blocked globally** [39]. 
*   **The Golden Rule:** Keep all raw concert clips strictly **≤ 59 seconds** (optimal range: **15–45 seconds**) [46].

### Copyright Enforcement Matrix [40]

| Enforcement Type | Trigger Condition in EDM [40] | Channel Impact [40] | Monetization Status [40] | Mitigation Workflow [40] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **In-App Shorts Audio** | Adding audio via YouTube Shorts tool (up to 60s) [40]. | Fully compliant; no channel penalty [40]. | Revenue split via Shorts Creator Pool [40]. | Ideal for adding studio audio over live laser visuals [40]. |
| **Standard Content ID Claim** | Live audio detected in raw venue clips (<60s) [40]. | No strike; video remains live globally [40]. | Ad revenue assigned to track's record label [40]. | Keep raw festival recordings strictly under 60 seconds [40]. |
| **Global Block (61–180s)** | Content ID match on vertical clips longer than 60 seconds [40]. | Video blocked worldwide; reduces channel reach [40]. | Unmonetized; hidden from public feeds [40]. | Upload as **Unlisted** first to test for claims before going Public [40]. |

## 5. End-to-End Upload & Publishing SOP [49]

Follow these seven steps for every upload to guarantee technical compliance, high-resolution rendering, and maximum copyright safety [49].

1.  **Export:** Produce a master 1080x1920 MP4 file (H.264, 60fps, AAC 320kbps, duration ≤59s) [49].
2.  **Upload:** Upload to YouTube Studio via desktop or mobile and set visibility to **Unlisted** [49].
3.  **Metadata:** Insert SEO Title and the Description Template (including rights disclaimers and tags) [49].
4.  **Processing:** Wait 30–60 minutes for automated Content ID checks and HD/VP09 processing to complete [49].
5.  **Audit:** Check the Restrictions column in YouTube Studio. Verify there are no "Blocked" or "Partially Blocked" status indicators [49].
6.  **Launch:** Transition visibility from Unlisted to **Public** or set a future **Schedule** [49].
7.  **Engagement:** Pin the first comment immediately to drive community interaction [49].
