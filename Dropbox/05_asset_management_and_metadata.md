# Doc 5: Unified Asset Management & Metadata Taxonomies

This document unifies your file storage, metadata conventions, and community moderations. By resolving the overlapping elements between your 6-tier and 3-folder architectures, it establishes a single, friction-free repository and engagement strategy.

---

## 1. Unified 4-Folder Hybrid Drive Architecture [11, 19]

This hybrid structure balances the technical integrity of the 6-tier high-end storage system with the speed and simplicity of the 3-folder layout, providing a scalable storage ecosystem [11, 19].

```
📁 Google Drive (Central Root)
├── 📂 01_RAW_INBOX (Color: Red) [11, 19]
├── 📂 02_IN_PROGRESS (Color: Orange) [11, 19]
├── 📂 03_READY_TO_POST (Color: Green) [11, 19]
└── 📂 04_ARCHIVE (Color: Gray) [11]
```

### Folder Configurations & Color Codes [11, 19]
1.  **`01_RAW_INBOX` (Color: Red):** For raw, unprocessed mobile captures or raw stream rips, organized internally by Event Name [11, 19].
2.  **`02_IN_PROGRESS` (Color: Orange):** Houses active DaVinci, Premiere, or CapCut project files, isolated audio stems (Demucs outputs), and denoised master clips [11, 19].
3.  **`03_READY_TO_POST` (Color: Green):** Stores platform-ready, watermarked MP4 exports with finalized text overlays [11, 19].
4.  **`04_ARCHIVE` (Color: Gray):** Deep compressed backups of Tiers 01 and 02 to maintain raw asset history without polluting your day-to-day workspace [11].

### Directory Guardrails [12]
*   **The Nesting Rule:** No sub-folder under any tier shall exceed **50 items** to prevent catalog indexing slowdowns and file browsing fatigue [12].
*   **Standard File Naming Syntax:** 
    > `YYYYMMDD_[Event]_[Artist]_[TrackName-or-ID]_V[#]_[Resolution].mp4` [12, 19]
    *   *Example:* `20260821_EDCOrlando_JohnSummit_WhereYouAre_V1_1080p.mp4`
