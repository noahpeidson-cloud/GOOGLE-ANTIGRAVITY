## 1. Brand Identity Matrix

To capture electronic dance music culture comprehensively, we operate under two distinct brand umbrellas. This dual-identity approach ensures we can cover high-energy visual spectacles as well as deep emotional club sets with absolute stylistic precision.

| Brand Aspect | Laser Baptism [28] | Music Baptism [16] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Resonance** | Focused primarily on high-energy visual/laser synchronization [16, 28]. | Total acoustic immersion and emotional transcendence [16]. |

### Visual Identity Descriptions
*   **Laser Baptism:** Neon laser beams slicing through a high-contrast synthesizer wave on a deep black background [33]. This represents the ultimate intersection of state-of-the-art visual production and massive crowd energy.
*   **Music Baptism:** Deep acoustic immersion focused on raw artist energy, intimate DJ deck POV shots, and sweeping warehouse light rigs.

## 3. Spark Orchestration Engine Architecture [1]

The **Spark Orchestration Engine** is an automated, 24/7 background AI agent ecosystem that integrates with Google Workspace, developer interfaces, and web platforms [1]. It coordinates files, competitive scrapers, and automated pre-production mapping [1].

```
Raw Phone Capture
      │
      ▼ (MCP Bridge Ingestion)
[ Google Drive / Storage ] ──▶ [ Metadata Routing Scripts ] ──▶ [ Specialized Folders ]
                                                                       │
                                                                       ▼
[ Automated Publishing ] ◀── [ Blogger Editor / Web ] ◀── [ AI Storyboarding / Google Flow ]
```

### Stage 1: Automated Asset Ingestion & Routing [2]
*   **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** Connects mobile upload applications directly to your central Google Drive/Cloud storage without manual intervention [2].
*   **Metadata Routing:** A background Python script listens for new file uploads and extracts embedded camera metadata (XMP/EXIF), location GPS markers, timestamps, and exposure profiles [2].
*   **Sorting Logic:** The script parses these parameters to automatically create and route raw clips to specific event folders (e.g., `/workspace/storage/01_INBOX_RAW/Outside Lands_Night_Footage/`) [2].

### Stage 2: Competitive Intelligence Tracker [3]
*   **Data Scraper Tooling:** Automated scripts scrape platform search feeds for trending audio, track IDs, view velocities, and competitive engagement rates from both YouTube Shorts and TikTok [3].
*   **GPT Workspace Integration:** Raw numbers are fed directly into structured spreadsheets where the GPT Workspace integration parses the trends [3].
*   **Weekly Action Summary:** The analysis engine produces a weekly action summary indicating which visual transition styles, specific tracks, and hashtags are driving the highest algorithmic reach [3].

### Stage 3: Storyboarding & Automated Publishing [4]
*   **Google Flow Storyboarding:** Prior to arriving at a venue, we feed Google Flow historical artist track trends and tempo databases to generate precise shot lists, visual scene maps, and track timelines [4].
*   **Blogger Editor Integration:** The engine automatically drafts corresponding articles containing vertical video previews, search-indexed text blocks, and contextual links, pushing them directly to the Blogger Editor (`https://www.blogger.com/blog/post/edit/...`) [4].
